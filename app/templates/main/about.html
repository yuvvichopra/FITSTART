About FitStart

Personalized fitness & nutrition engineering platform designed for real-world constraints

Project Background & Vision

FitStart was designed and engineered by a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (AI/ML) student to bridge the gap between academic exercise physiology and accessible software. Most commercial fitness applications provide either generic static workouts or hallucinatory AI routines that fail to account for collegiate budget limits, hostel mess constraints, and safety guidelines.

FitStart uses a deterministic, transparent rules-based recommendation engine paired with verified physiological formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, Epley 1RM, ACSM FITT principles, and ICMR-NIN nutritional standards).

Core Engineering Principles

1. Evidence Over Hype

Every rep range, set volume, and macro ratio links to peer-reviewed literature rather than social media influencers.

2. Hostel & Student Friendly

Realistic options for students living in hostels with mess meals, limited cooking facilities, and vegetarian/eggetarian diets.

3. Safety & Joint Preservation

Pain-movement rules automatically substitute compromising exercises (e.g. knee or lower back discomfort) with biomechanically safer alternatives.

4. Data Isolation & Security

Strict user session verification, CSRF defense, and database relationship isolation prevent cross-tenant data access.

Stack: Python 3.14 • Flask 3 • SQLAlchemy ORM • MySQL / SQLite • Chart.js • HTML5 / CSS3
